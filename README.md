# 📊 Retail Customer Cohort Analysis | Power BI

## 📌  Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail customer behavior using **Microsoft Power BI**. The interactive dashboard provides insights into customer retention, churn, recovery, and acquisition through cohort analysis and monthly trend visualization.

The goal is to understand how customer engagement changes over time, identify potential customer loss, and support data-driven business decisions to improve customer loyalty and long-term retention.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer behavior across monthly cohorts.
- Evaluate customer retention and churn patterns over time.
- Track new, retained, recovered, and lost customers.
- Compare customer activity across different cohort periods.
- Identify opportunities to improve customer retention and reactivation.
- Support strategic decision-making through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Data Transformation** — Data preparation and cleaning
- **Cohort Analysis** — Customer behavior tracking across periods
- **Data Analytics** — Trend analysis and business insights

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **Cohort Performance Matrix:** Compares customer activity across monthly cohorts and subsequent periods.
- **Customer Retention Breakdown:** Visualizes the composition of new, recovered, and retained customers.
- **Customer Churn Analysis:** Tracks monthly lost-customer trends to identify potential attrition patterns.
- **Customer Recovery Analysis:** Monitors recovered customers over time.
- **Customer Acquisition Trends:** Evaluates changes in new-customer counts.
- **Interactive Filters and Selection:** Allows users to explore different cohort measures and customer metrics.

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. **Cohort Performance:** The dashboard reveals differences in customer activity across monthly cohorts, helping evaluate customer engagement over time.
2. **Retention Patterns:** The proportions of new, recovered, and retained customers vary across periods, highlighting the importance of ongoing customer engagement.
3. **Customer Churn:** Lost-customer counts fluctuate over time, with several noticeable spikes that may warrant further investigation.
4. **Customer Recovery:** Recovered customers contribute to customer activity in multiple periods, indicating an opportunity for targeted win-back campaigns.
5. **Acquisition Trends:** New-customer counts change over time, emphasizing the need to monitor acquisition alongside customer retention.

*Note: These insights are based on the dashboard visualization. Exact retention rates, churn percentages, and business impact should be validated using the underlying dataset and measure definitions.*

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Develop targeted retention strategies for customers at risk of leaving.
- Investigate the causes of unusually high customer losses.
- Implement personalized campaigns to reactivate inactive customers.
- Monitor acquisition channels and evaluate customer acquisition performance.
- Track cohort retention over time to assess long-term customer engagement.
- Use customer behavior insights to support data-driven marketing decisions.


## 📊 Business Value

This dashboard demonstrates how cohort analysis and business intelligence can help retail organizations understand customer lifecycle patterns, monitor customer retention, investigate churn, and identify opportunities to improve customer relationships.

## 🏁 Conclusion

The Retail Customer Cohort Analysis project showcases the practical application of Power BI in retail analytics. By combining cohort performance, retention breakdowns, and customer trend analysis, the dashboard transforms customer data into actionable insights that can support customer relationship management and business growth.
